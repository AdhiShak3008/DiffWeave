MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT (NDA)
=======================================================
Agreement ID: NDA-2026-MUTUAL-01
Parties: NovaTech Solutions Inc. and Apex Retail Corp.
Effective Date: January 15, 2026
Term: 2 Years from Effective Date

1. CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION
Confidential Information includes all proprietary software architectures, source code, trade secrets, customer financial records, and commercial negotiations disclosed by either party.

2. NON-DISCLOSURE OBLIGATIONS
Each Receiving Party agrees:
- To protect Confidential Information with the same degree of care as its own proprietary assets (minimum reasonable care).
- To restrict access solely to employees and certified legal advisors with a strict need-to-know.
- Not to disclose or reverse engineer any proprietary materials without prior written consent.

3. EXCLUSIONS FROM CONFIDENTIALITY
Information is excluded if it is:
- Already known to the recipient prior to disclosure.
- Lawfully obtained from an independent third party without confidentiality breach.
- Required to be disclosed by valid subpoena or court order with immediate notice to Disclosing Party.

4. RETURN OR DESTRUCTION
Upon written request, all copies of Confidential Information must be permanently destroyed or returned within 10 calendar days.
