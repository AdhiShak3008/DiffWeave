AMENDMENT NO. 1 TO MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT
=======================================================
Amendment ID: AMD-2026-CLOUD-01A
Reference Agreement: MSA-2026-CLOUD-01
Effective Date: April 15, 2026
Parties: NovaTech Solutions Inc. and Apex Retail Corp.

WHEREAS, the Parties agree to amend specific operational and commercial terms of the Master Services Agreement as follows:

1. REVISED SUBSCRIPTION FEES
- Section 2 is amended: Monthly Subscription Fee is reduced to $4,200 USD in consideration of a 24-month commitment.
- Payment Terms remain Net 30 days from invoice date.

2. ENHANCED SLA COMMITMENT
- Section 3 is amended: Monthly Uptime Guarantee is increased from 99.5% to 99.9% 24/7 availability.
- Service Credits are increased to 10% credit for any month falling below the 99.9% threshold.

3. EXPANDED LIABILITY CAP
- Section 4 is amended: Aggregate Liability Cap is increased from $60,000 USD to 2x annual contract value ($100,800 USD).

4. EXTENDED TERMINATION NOTICE
- Section 5 is amended: Termination for convenience now requires 60 days prior written notice (previously 30 days).

5. DATA PRIVACY AND SECURITY ADDENDUM
- Provider shall undergo an annual SOC 2 Type II audit and report critical security incidents within 24 hours of discovery.
