MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT (MSA)
=======================================================
Agreement ID: MSA-2026-CLOUD-01
Parties: NovaTech Solutions Inc. ("Provider") and Apex Retail Corp. ("Customer")
Effective Date: March 1, 2026
Initial Term: 12 Months

1. SCOPE OF SERVICES
Provider will deliver managed cloud infrastructure and real-time inventory synchronization software to Customer.

2. FEES AND PAYMENT TERMS
- Monthly Subscription Fee: $5,000 USD billed at the start of each month.
- Payment Terms: Net 30 days from invoice date. Late payments incur a 1.5% monthly interest fee.

3. SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT (SLA)
- Monthly Uptime Guarantee: 99.5% service availability across standard operating hours.
- Maintenance Window: Planned maintenance scheduled exclusively on Sundays between 02:00 and 04:00 UTC.
- Service Credits: Customer receives a 5% billing credit for each full 1% drop below the monthly uptime target.

4. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
- Aggregate Liability Cap: Strictly capped at 1x the total annual fees paid under this Agreement ($60,000 USD).
- Consequential Damages: Neither party shall be liable for indirect, incidental, or lost-profit damages.

5. TERMINATION AND NOTICE
- Termination for Convenience: Either party may terminate with 30 days prior written notice.
- Governing Law: State of Delaware, United States.
