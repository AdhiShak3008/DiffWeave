CLINICAL TRIAL PROTOCOL AMENDMENT: CARDIOVASCULAR REPAIR
=============================================================
Protocol ID: PROTOCOL-CR-2026-01-AMD1
Sponsor: Global BioHealth Institute
Amendment Effective Date: August 15, 2026
Reference: Protocol PROTOCOL-CR-2026-01 dated February 1, 2026

This Amendment formally updates and revises the clinical trial parameters:

1. REVISED PATIENT ENROLLMENT
- Total Planned Enrollment: Enrollment is expanded from 1,200 patients to a total of 1,800 adult patients to increase statistical power across 50 international sites.
- Target Patient Age: Revised to adults aged 60 years or older (expanded from previous 65+ cutoff).

2. UPDATED DOSAGE AND MEDICATION REGIMEN
- Post-Operative Anticoagulation: Patients will now receive direct oral anticoagulants (Apixaban 5 mg twice daily) instead of clopidogrel, maintaining aspirin 81 mg daily.

3. REVISED PRIMARY OUTCOME MEASURES
- Primary Efficacy Endpoint: Transvalvular mean gradient threshold is revised to under 12 mmHg at 12-month follow-up (upgraded from 15 mmHg).
