ARTISAN SOURDOUGH BREAD BAKING GUIDE
===================================================
Document ID: KITCHEN-BAKE-2026-01
Author: Traditional Bakery Workshop
Category: Culinary Arts & Baking

1. INGREDIENTS AND PROPORTIONS
- Bread Flour: 500 grams of unbleached high-protein organic white flour.
- Water: 375 grams of lukewarm filtered water (75% hydration).
- Active Sourdough Starter: 100 grams of bubbly 100% hydration starter.
- Fine Sea Salt: 10 grams (2% of total flour weight).

2. FERMENTATION AND PROOFING SCHEDULE
- Autolyse: Mix flour and water and rest for 45 minutes before adding starter and salt.
- Bulk Fermentation: Perform 4 sets of stretch-and-folds spaced 30 minutes apart at 24 degrees Celsius.
- Cold Retard: Place shaped batard in a banneton basket and ferment in refrigerator at 4 degrees Celsius for 14 hours.

3. BAKING INSTRUCTIONS
- Oven Temperature: Preheat Dutch oven at 230 degrees Celsius (450 degrees Fahrenheit) for 45 minutes.
- Bake Duration: Bake covered with lid for 20 minutes to trap steam, then remove lid and bake uncovered for 22 minutes until crust is deep golden brown.
