CLINICAL TRIAL PROTOCOL: CARDIOVASCULAR REPAIR
=======================================================
Protocol ID: PROTOCOL-CR-2026-01
Sponsor: Global BioHealth Institute
Study Phase: Phase III Multi-Center Randomized Trial
Effective Date: February 1, 2026

1. STUDY OBJECTIVE
This trial evaluates the safety and efficacy of the novel transcatheter bioprosthetic valve (AeroValve-X) in elderly patients diagnosed with severe symptomatic calcific aortic stenosis.

2. PATIENT COHORT AND ENROLLMENT
- Total Planned Enrollment: 1,200 adult patients across 35 clinical sites.
- Target Patient Age: Adults aged 65 years or older.
- Baseline Diagnostic Requirement: Aortic valve area strictly less than 0.8 cm2 measured via echocardiogram.

3. DOSAGE AND INTERVENTION PROTOCOL
- Surgical Intervention: Implantation of the AeroValve-X bioprosthetic valve via transfemoral approach.
- Post-Operative Anticoagulation: Patients receive daily low-dose aspirin (81 mg) combined with clopidogrel for 6 months post-procedure.

4. PRIMARY OUTCOME MEASURES
- Primary Safety Endpoint: All-cause mortality rate within 30 days of procedure.
- Primary Efficacy Endpoint: Significant reduction in transvalvular mean gradient to under 15 mmHg at 12-month follow-up.
